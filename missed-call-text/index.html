What else it handles

They hang up before the greeting ends

No text goes out (that's the law). You get this, and one tap in the CRM sends the text if you want:

Missed call from (850) 555-0143 at 2:31 PM. They hung up before the greeting ended, so no text went out. Send it: btb-btp-crm.vercel.app/calls/...
Family, suppliers and regulars

Numbers on your skip list never get questions, only:

B&T Pressure Washing: Sorry we missed your call. Taylor will call you back soon. Reply STOP to opt out.
They'd rather leave a message

They press 2 and talk. You get a text with a link, and it plays on the lead page. No text goes to them.

A landline or a hidden number

Those can't get texts, so none is sent. For a landline you get a text to call them back.

STOP, or "stop texting me"

Either one stops every text to that number right away, and you're told. Calls from them still reach you.

It never sells or promises

No prices, deals, links or arrival times you haven't confirmed. If it's ever unsure, it thanks them and hands the lead to you.

Off in ten seconds

A pause switch in the CRM, and your carrier's code turns forwarding off. Your number never changes.
